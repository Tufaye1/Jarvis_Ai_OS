iPhone 17 Pro / Pro Max Inspector

Work top to bottom with the seller present. Any red Deal-breaker failure means walk away. Negotiate failures mean the phone is real but worth less.

Model being inspected:
Limits of this tool. No website or app outside Apple can read the phone's hardware serials or prove authenticity by itself. The strongest proofs are: Apple's own coverage page accepting the serial, the phone being recognised by a Mac/PC (Finder or Apple Devices), Parts and Service History in Settings, and a clean erase + setup with no Activation Lock. This tool organises those checks and catches mismatches. Your eyes still do the work.
